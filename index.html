How to Use the Fermentation Brine Calculator

Getting the salt-to-water ratio exactly right is the most critical step in wild lacto-fermentation. This free online brine calculator uses precision weight metrics in grams to eliminate the guesswork caused by variations in salt crystal sizes across different brands (like Kosher vs. sea salt).

Standard Salinity Guidelines:

  • 2.0% - 2.5%: Ideal for soft vegetables like cucumbers, hot peppers, zucchini, and traditional sauerkraut.
  • 3.0% - 5.0%: Best for firm vegetables, warm environments, or long-term aging (such as whole root vegetables or olives).

Simply input your water volume, choose your preferred target salinity percentage, and weigh out the exact salt requirement on a digital kitchen scale for safe, mold-free fermenting every single time.
