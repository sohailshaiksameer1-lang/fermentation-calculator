How to Use These Precision Ratios

Getting your ingredient weights exactly right prevents failure in both wild lacto-fermentation and artisan baking. Standard culinary measurements like teaspoons or cups are highly inaccurate because different ingredient brands have varying crystal sizes and flour densities.

Fermentation Brine Guidelines

Always base your percentages on the weight of the water. Standard vegetable ferments like cucumbers or chilis excel at a 2.0% to 2.5% concentration, whereas firm root vegetables benefit from a 3.0% to 5.0% brine.

Sourdough Hydration Matrix

Hydration determines the open structure and crumb of your artisan bread. A lower hydration profile (65%) yields dough that is structural and easy to shape. High hydration doughs (75%+) produce massive, glassy air bubbles but require advanced stretch-and-fold techniques.
